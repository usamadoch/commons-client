Cancellation & Refund Policy
Last Updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview
Commons is a digital platform that allows users to support creators through paid memberships and one-time payments during live streams. All payments on Commons are processed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR), unless otherwise indicated at checkout.
This policy explains how subscription cancellations, refund requests, and payment disputes are handled on Commons.

2. Subscription Cancellations
Users can cancel their recurring creator memberships at any time through their Commons account dashboard.
No cancellation fee: Users will not be charged a fee for cancelling a membership.
Continued access: After cancellation, users retain access to the membership's paid content and benefits until the end of their current billing period.
Future payments: Once cancellation takes effect, the membership will not renew, and no further recurring payments will be charged for that membership.
Partial refunds: Cancelling a membership does not automatically entitle the user to a refund for the current billing period or any unused portion of the subscription.

3. Refund Eligibility
Because Commons provides immediate access to digital content and membership benefits, payments are generally non-refundable once access has been granted.
However, users may contact our support team to request a refund in the following circumstances:
Duplicate payments: The same subscription or purchase was charged more than once in error.
Payment errors: A payment was processed incorrectly or charged in the wrong amount.
Technical issues: A user paid for a membership but could not access the purchased benefits, and the issue could not be resolved.
Unauthorized transactions: A payment appears to have been made without the account holder's authorization. Such requests will be reviewed before a decision is made.
Other applicable circumstances: Refunds may be provided where required by applicable law.
Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. Each request will be reviewed based on the transaction details and circumstances.

4. One-Time Payments and Super Messages
Super Messages are one-time payments that allow users to highlight messages during a creator's livestream.
These payments are generally non-refundable once successfully processed and delivered. Users should verify the amount and recipient before completing a payment.
Refund requests may be considered for duplicate charges, payment errors, failed delivery, or other eligible circumstances described in this policy.

5. How to Request a Refund
To request a refund or report a billing issue, contact us at support@commons.pk.
Please include:
Your registered email address
The transaction date and amount
The relevant creator or membership, if applicable
Your transaction reference, if available
A brief explanation of the issue
Our support team aims to respond to inquiries within 48 hours. We may request additional information to investigate a claim.

6. Refund Processing Time
If a refund is approved, Commons will initiate the refund through the applicable payment method or payment provider.
Approved refunds are generally expected to be processed within 5–10 business days, although the time required for the funds to appear in the user's account may vary depending on the payment provider or issuing bank.
We will communicate any material delays where possible.

7. Billing Disputes
If you believe a payment was processed incorrectly, please contact us at support@commons.pk so we can investigate and attempt to resolve the issue.
Users retain any rights available to them under applicable law and the rules of their payment provider.
